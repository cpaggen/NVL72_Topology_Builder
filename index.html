NVL72 compute-fabric model
18 compute trays × 4 GPUs per tray = 72 GPUs per rack. There are 4 GPU-position rails in each of 2 independent planes. Each GPU connects to both planes at 400G per plane. Leaf switches are sized from the port and bandwidth inputs; a rail is not assumed to equal one switch.

Plane and rail allocation
